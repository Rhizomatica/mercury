# N3JXB audio, 2026-11-16 (#607 evidence)

Spectrograms of N3JXB's own station recording (one channel: his
transmissions and what his receiver heard), 48 kHz FLAC shown at 8 kHz.
Each file was aligned to his Mercury log by his own keydowns: in file 1 all
23 of them match the log's PTT ON/OFF to within a second.  Times are his
local time (UTC-5).

- `1_before_16-08-50_to_16-12-30.png`: the end of file 1.  Doug's MFSK500
  CALL and polls (clean tones on a black background), VE4KLM's MFSK500
  answers, and a steady carrier near 1 kHz 16:12:03-16:12:17.  No dense
  traffic yet.
- `2_hold_16-13-11_to_16-15-41.png` and `3_hold_16-15-31_to_16-17-59.png`:
  file 2.  Doug's last keydown (16:13:26-16:13:53), then a dense signal over
  300-1750 Hz that never stops.  Mercury held 16:14:28.8-16:16:25.1
  ("a frame's preamble was caught"), then declared the link lost.
- `4_zoom_4s_during_the_hold.png`: 4 s of it.  Multi-tone data blocks of
  ~4.7 s and a short (~0.4 s) wideband burst about every 5.2 s: another data
  exchange on the frequency, not noise.
