# Mercury privacy policy

This covers Mercury, the HF modem, in every form Rhizomatica distributes it:
the iOS and Android apps, and the Linux, Windows and macOS builds.

**Rhizomatica collects no data from Mercury.** The app has no account, no
analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no tracking, and it sends
nothing to Rhizomatica or to anyone else on the internet.

## What the app uses, and where it stays

- **The microphone, or your radio's audio input.** Mercury is a modem: it
  listens to the audio from your radio to decode the signal, as it arrives.
  The audio is not recorded, stored or sent anywhere.
- **The speaker, or your radio's audio output**, to transmit the modem
  signal.
- **Files on your device.** Mercury keeps its settings, a log of what the
  modem did (callsigns, times, signal reports) and your chat history on
  the device. They stay there until you delete them or the app.
- **Local network ports.** Mercury opens TCP ports so that the email,
  chat and file applications you choose can use the modem, and a web page
  for its status. They answer to programs on your device or local network,
  not to Rhizomatica.

## What goes over the air

What you transmit is a radio signal. Anyone with a receiver can hear it, and
amateur-radio rules in most countries forbid encrypting it. Your callsign
is sent with every connection. Do not send over the air anything you would
not want others to read.
